Inside the café

Free-roaming cats. Zero rush.

Rescued Treasures is a relaxed, welcoming space where adoptable cats and kittens wander, nap and say hello on their own terms. Complimentary Wi-Fi, snacks and drinks are included, and there are special events through the year.

About the initiative
Get involved

Help a cat get home.

Adopting is the big one. Fostering, volunteering and giving keep the whole rescue running.

Adopt

Meet the cats at the café, apply, and complete an in-person interview. Applicants must be 21 or older.

Foster

Give a cat a quiet place to recover. Most placements last two weeks to two months. Ages 21 and up.

Forever home, please?

Volunteer

Join the Cat Care Crew, help in the café, drive cats to the vet and more. Ages 16 and up, orientation first.

Donate

Gifts fund medical care, food and supplies. You can also give in kind or sponsor a cat.
