How adoption works

From hello to home.

  1. 01
    Visit the caféMeet the cats in person. PALNV doesn't offer pre-adoptions.
  2. 02
    ApplySubmit an adoption application for the cat who chose you.
  3. 03
    In-person interviewChat with the team. Please allow up to 24 hours for approval.
  4. 04
    Sign and go homeSign the adoption contract once approved, and bring a carrier.

A non-refundable hold deposit may be placed after meeting a cat and applying. Holds are valid for three café business days. Adoptions are first-come, first-served.

Client to confirmThe adoption page counts café business days as Thursday to Sunday, but the café also opens on Wednesdays. Age bands come from PALNV's published categories. Fees are deliberately left out of this concept.

Every cat goes home

  • Spayed or neutered
  • Up to date on rabies and FVRCP vaccines
  • Microchipped
  • Dewormed
  • Tested and treated for fecal parasites
  • FIV and FeLV tested

Bring with you

  • Valid photo ID (applicants must be 21+)
  • Landlord approval or pet lease addendum, if you rent
  • Consent from any spouse or roommates
  • A pet carrier, if you plan to adopt that day
